# Accounting Skill

Translate reliable bookkeeping records into consistent financial recognition, measurement, close and reporting for **Bzync Software Development Services** and its projects.

Use professional accounting judgment conservatively. Do not fabricate compliance conclusions, accounting standards applicability, tax rates or filing requirements.

## Separation from bookkeeping

```text
book-keeping-skill
  = capture + classify candidates + post source activity + reconcile

accounting-skill
  = recognition policy + measurement + accrual/deferral + close + statements + analysis
```

Accounting may require adjusting entries after bookkeeping is complete.

## Core responsibilities

- accounting-policy definition and consistent application;
- accruals and cut-off;
- revenue recognition and deferred/unearned revenue;
- expense recognition and prepaid expenses;
- capitalization vs expense analysis;
- fixed assets, useful lives and depreciation/amortization policy;
- provisions/allowances when applicable;
- foreign-currency accounting and FX differences;
- inter-account/entity reconciliation where applicable;
- month-end and year-end close;
- trial balance review;
- income statement, balance sheet and cash-flow preparation/mapping;
- management accounts and variance analysis;
- audit schedules and evidence traceability;
- accounting controls for software/billing platforms.

## Accounting decision frame

For every material treatment identify:

```text
transaction/event
substance/economic purpose
applicable reporting/tax framework
recognition point
measurement basis
accounts affected
period/cut-off
supporting evidence
estimates/judgment
reversal/settlement behavior
required disclosure or management note
```

If the applicable reporting framework is unknown, say so and determine it before asserting treatment.

## Software / SaaS / cloud context

Pay particular attention to:

- prepaid annual/quarterly customer subscriptions;
- deferred/unearned service revenue;
- setup/implementation/professional-service components;
- discounts, credits and refunds;
- payment gateway fees;
- infrastructure/cloud costs and gross-margin classification;
- domain/reseller pass-through costs;
- customer deposits and refundable balances;
- foreign-currency billing and settlement;
- capitalizable equipment and infrastructure assets;
- internally developed software only when the relevant framework and policy support treatment.

A billing schedule, cash receipt and accounting revenue schedule are separate concepts.

## Close discipline

```text
bookkeeping complete
 -> subledgers reconciled
 -> cut-off review
 -> accruals/deferrals
 -> fixed assets/depreciation
 -> FX/revaluation where applicable
 -> tax/accounting adjustments
 -> trial balance review
 -> financial statements
 -> analytical review
 -> approval
 -> period lock
```

Do not reopen a closed period merely to make current reports look cleaner. Use controlled adjustments and documented reopening when truly required.

## Philippine compliance awareness

When work touches Philippine statutory/tax/accounting requirements:

- identify the entity/business form and applicable reporting framework instead of assuming it;
- verify current BIR/SEC/other official requirements and effective dates;
- do not hardcode current tax rates/thresholds/forms without official verification;
- distinguish financial accounting from tax accounting;
- flag matters that require a Philippine CPA, tax professional or legal adviser.
