# Accounting Policy Governance

A policy should state:

```text
policy name
purpose
scope
reporting framework / authority
effective date
recognition rule
measurement rule
materiality/threshold rule when authorized
accounts and dimensions
required evidence
estimation method
review frequency
owner/approver
version history
```

Avoid embedding accounting policy solely in application code. Where a rule can change, version configuration and preserve the policy used for historical records.

## Policy changes

For a material change:

- identify whether it is a policy change, estimate change or correction;
- assess historical impact under the applicable framework;
- plan migration/recalculation only when appropriate;
- preserve audit traceability;
- document approval and effective period.
