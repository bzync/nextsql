# Financial Statement Mapping

## Income statement

At minimum reason about:

```text
Revenue
- Cost of revenue / direct costs
= Gross profit
- Operating expenses
= Operating result
+/- Other income/expense
= Profit/loss before tax
- Tax where applicable
= Net profit/loss
```

Use presentation appropriate to the applicable reporting framework and entity.

## Balance sheet

```text
Assets
  current
  non-current
Liabilities
  current
  non-current
Equity
```

Balances must reconcile to the underlying ledger and supporting schedules.

## Cash flow

Do not derive cash flow by intuition. Map cash movements under the applicable framework/method and reconcile opening cash + net movement = closing cash.

## Management reporting

Useful operating views can include:

- recurring vs non-recurring revenue;
- revenue by product/segment;
- direct infrastructure/service-delivery cost;
- gross margin;
- operating expense by function;
- cash runway;
- receivables aging;
- deferred revenue;
- committed vendor/infrastructure cost.

Management dimensions must not corrupt statutory ledger integrity.
