# Month-End Close

## Pre-close

- confirm bookkeeping period completeness;
- reconcile bank/payment processors;
- reconcile AR/AP and material clearing accounts;
- review late/unposted transactions;
- identify unusual balances.

## Adjustments

Review as relevant:

- accrued expenses;
- accrued income;
- prepaid expenses;
- deferred/unearned revenue;
- depreciation/amortization;
- FX adjustments;
- doubtful/credit-loss considerations;
- payroll/tax liabilities;
- inventory only if the business actually holds inventory;
- intercompany/inter-unit balances if they exist.

## Analytical review

Compare current period to:

- prior month/year;
- budget/forecast;
- revenue drivers;
- gross margin/direct costs;
- headcount/operating expenses;
- cash movement.

Investigate significant variances instead of merely documenting percentages.

## Close completion

- approved adjustments posted;
- trial balance reviewed;
- statements produced;
- key reconciliations signed off;
- open issues logged;
- period locked;
- management pack retained with version/evidence.
