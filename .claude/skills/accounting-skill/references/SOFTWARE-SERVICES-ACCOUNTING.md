# Software / Services Accounting Considerations

This is a decision checklist, not a universal accounting conclusion.

## Customer contracts

Determine:

- what was promised;
- service period;
- billing frequency;
- refund/cancellation rights;
- setup/professional services;
- credits/discounts;
- variable usage charges;
- taxes/withholding;
- currency.

Separate:

```text
contract/order state
invoice state
payment state
service-delivery state
revenue-recognition state
```

## Direct costs

For cloud/platform products, determine and consistently define which costs are direct cost of revenue versus operating expense. Examples may include customer-attributable infrastructure, third-party reseller costs, support/service-delivery labor or shared platform overhead depending on policy.

## Deferred balances

Longer prepaid service periods often require a schedule rather than immediate full-period recognition. Do not infer the schedule without the accounting policy/framework.

## Refunds / credits

Keep original billings and subsequent credits/refunds traceable. Avoid mutating historical invoices or recognized periods without controlled accounting treatment.
