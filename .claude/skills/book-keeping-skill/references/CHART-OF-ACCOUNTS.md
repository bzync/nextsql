# Chart of Accounts Guidance

Do not create hundreds of accounts merely to encode every product, project or customer. Use dimensions/cost centers when the accounting system supports them.

Typical top-level classes:

```text
Assets
Liabilities
Equity
Revenue
Cost of Revenue / Cost of Sales
Operating Expenses
Other Income / Expense
```

For a software/cloud/services business, potentially useful dimensions include:

- legal/business unit;
- product line;
- workspace/project;
- customer segment;
- department/function;
- region;
- recurring vs professional-services revenue;
- direct infrastructure cost vs shared overhead.

The exact chart of accounts is an accounting-policy decision. `accounting-skill` should review material account design.
