# Business Operating System

Keep company execution connected from strategy to measurable operations.

## Cadence

A lightweight operating cadence can include:

### Weekly
- sales pipeline and collections;
- customer incidents/retention risks;
- service capacity;
- major delivery blockers;
- cash-sensitive commitments.

### Monthly
- actual vs plan revenue/cost/cash;
- gross/contribution margin;
- customer acquisition/retention;
- product/support workload;
- infrastructure/vendor utilization;
- strategic initiative progress;
- risk register.

### Quarterly
- pricing/packaging review;
- customer segment performance;
- roadmap and capital allocation;
- vendor/build-vs-buy decisions;
- scenario/forecast refresh.

## KPI rule

Every KPI needs:

```text
definition
formula
source of truth
owner
cadence
target/threshold
decision it informs
```

Avoid dashboards filled with metrics that do not trigger decisions.
