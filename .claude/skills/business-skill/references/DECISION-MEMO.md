# Business Decision Memo

For material pricing, product, vendor, expansion or investment decisions use:

```text
Decision
Why now
Objective
Facts
Assumptions
Options
Customer impact
Revenue/cost/cash impact
Operational impact
Technology impact
Compliance dependencies
Risks/mitigations
Recommendation
Milestones
Success metrics
Revisit/stop trigger
```

A decision memo should make future review possible: what did we believe, and what evidence would cause us to change course?
