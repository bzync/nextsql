# Go-To-Market

## Start with a defined segment

Document:

- who has the problem;
- urgency/frequency;
- current alternative;
- decision maker/user/payer;
- proof needed to trust Bzync;
- expected sales cycle;
- onboarding friction;
- expansion/renewal path.

## Funnel

Use stage definitions tied to evidence, for example:

```text
identified lead
 -> qualified problem
 -> qualified fit/budget
 -> proposal/trial
 -> commercial commitment
 -> paid/onboarded
 -> activated
 -> retained/expanded
```

Do not inflate pipeline by treating every contact as a qualified opportunity.

## Feedback loop

Sales/support/product/engineering should share structured reasons for:

- wins;
- losses;
- churn;
- failed onboarding;
- support escalation;
- requested capabilities;
- pricing objections.
