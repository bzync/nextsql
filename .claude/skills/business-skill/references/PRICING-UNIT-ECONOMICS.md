# Pricing and Unit Economics

## Per-offer model

Estimate or measure:

```text
list price
realized price after discounts
payment/transaction costs
variable infrastructure/vendor cost
service-delivery/support cost
refund/credit/failed-payment allowance
contribution amount
contribution margin
cash collection timing
```

Then consider shared operating costs separately.

## Subscription products

Useful measures may include:

- MRR/ARR with precise definitions;
- new/expansion/contraction/churned recurring revenue;
- logo and revenue retention;
- ARPU/ARPA;
- support cost per account;
- infrastructure cost per active resource/account;
- gross margin;
- CAC/payback when acquisition data is reliable.

Do not use SaaS metrics mechanically for project-based software services.

## Discounts

Model the total contract impact, not just percentage off:

- contract term;
- payment timing;
- minimum commitment;
- resource/utilization risk;
- support obligations;
- renewal anchor price;
- cancellation/refund terms.
