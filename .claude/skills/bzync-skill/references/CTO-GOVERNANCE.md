# CTO Governance

For high-impact decisions evaluate:

```text
customer/business value
speed to validated outcome
security/privacy risk
reliability/operational risk
engineering complexity
recurring cost
team cognitive load
vendor dependency
future optionality
migration/reversibility
```

## Require deeper review for

- new persistent database/storage technology;
- new runtime/service class;
- public network exposure;
- authentication/identity changes;
- regulated/sensitive data handling;
- irreversible migrations;
- cross-region replication;
- significant recurring infrastructure/vendor cost;
- broad framework/platform replacement.

## Decision record

For major choices capture:

```text
Objective
Current constraints
Options considered
Decision
Why this option
Risks and mitigations
Cost/operational impact
Migration/rollback
Revisit trigger
```

Avoid technology novelty as a goal. Prefer durable company capability and a coherent operational platform.
