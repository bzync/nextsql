# Technology Decision Review

Ask:

- Does this enable a customer/business outcome?
- Is this a one-way door or reversible?
- What must the team operate 24/7 afterward?
- What is the failure blast radius?
- What knowledge concentration/bus factor does it create?
- What are direct and hidden recurring costs?
- What compliance/security responsibilities are introduced?
- Could a simpler architecture satisfy the next 12–18 months?
- What metric/event should trigger scaling to the more complex option?
