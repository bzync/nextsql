# NIST CSF 2.0 Use in Bzync

NIST CSF 2.0 is a risk-management framework for organizations of any size/sector. Use it as an outcome taxonomy, not a prescriptive technology list.

## Practical profile

Maintain:

- Current Profile — what outcomes/controls/evidence exist now;
- Target Profile — desired outcomes based on risk/business needs;
- gaps prioritized by risk and dependency;
- owners, milestones and evidence.

## Governance emphasis

CSF 2.0 added/strengthened explicit governance and supply-chain focus. Connect technical security work to business ownership, policy, suppliers and enterprise risk rather than leaving it solely to developers.
