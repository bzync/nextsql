# Children and Sensitive/High-Risk Data

Treat this as an escalation category.

## Review areas

- age assurance/age gate appropriateness;
- parental/guardian involvement where legally required;
- child-appropriate notice/UX;
- profiling/advertising restrictions;
- data minimization;
- visibility/discovery defaults;
- safeguarding and abuse risks;
- retention;
- automated decisions;
- biometrics/location;
- third-party SDKs;
- human review and appeal where high-impact decisions occur.

Age thresholds and definitions differ by country and can change. Never encode a universal "under 13" or "under 16" rule across all jurisdictions.
