# Cross-Border Transfers and Vendors

## Vendor due diligence

Record:

- service and business purpose;
- role (processor/service provider/independent controller/etc. as applicable);
- data categories and subjects;
- processing/hosting/support locations;
- subprocessors;
- security posture and relevant assurance;
- incident notification commitment;
- retention/deletion/export capability;
- assistance with rights requests;
- transfer mechanism/terms where required;
- exit plan.

## Engineering rule

Do not integrate a vendor until the product can explain what data it sends, when, why, where and how deletion/export works.

## Subprocessor changes

For SaaS products, keep a mechanism to track and communicate subprocessor changes where contract/law requires it. Architecture should not make vendor replacement impossible without uncontrolled data residue.
