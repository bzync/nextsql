# Jurisdiction Resolution

## Do not infer solely from IP address

User location is only one signal. Applicability may turn on establishment, targeting/offer of goods/services, monitoring, residence, legal entity, contract, sector and other statutory tests.

## Source quality

Prefer regulator/government/official legislative sources. Guidance can change without the statute changing. Capture `verified_at` and effective dates.

## Examples of official authorities

- EU/EEA: EUR-Lex and European Data Protection Board / national supervisory authorities.
- California: California Privacy Protection Agency and California Attorney General.
- Singapore: Personal Data Protection Commission.
- Brazil: Agência Nacional de Proteção de Dados.
- India: India Code and Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology.
- Philippines: National Privacy Commission.
