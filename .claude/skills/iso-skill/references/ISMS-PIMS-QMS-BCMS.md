# ISMS / PIMS / QMS / BCMS Integration

## ISMS — information security

Focus on risk governance around confidentiality, integrity and availability of information/services, supported by real operational controls.

## PIMS — privacy

Connect privacy roles, processing inventory, privacy risk, data-subject rights, processor/controller governance and privacy controls to the ISMS and applicable law.

## QMS — quality

Connect customer requirements, repeatable delivery, defects, service performance, feedback, root-cause/corrective action and continual improvement.

## BCMS — continuity

Define critical services/processes, impact/tolerance, recovery strategy, dependencies, exercises and improvement. Backups without tested restoration and business recovery sequencing are insufficient.

## AI management

When AI systems are material, define ownership, intended use, data/model lifecycle, risk, human oversight, security/privacy, evaluation, monitoring and change control using the current applicable AI governance framework/standard.
