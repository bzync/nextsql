# ISO Governance Model

## One integrated management system where practical

Avoid separate bureaucracies for security, privacy, quality and continuity. Reuse compatible processes:

- document/version control;
- risk and opportunity management;
- objectives/metrics;
- competence/training;
- supplier management;
- internal audit;
- corrective action;
- management review;
- continual improvement.

Then retain domain-specific requirements where necessary.

## Governance records

At minimum maintain:

- scope and boundaries;
- roles/accountabilities;
- policy set;
- legal/regulatory/contractual obligations;
- asset/process/service inventories;
- risk register and treatment decisions;
- objectives and measurements;
- exceptions/accepted risks;
- internal audit program/results;
- nonconformity/corrective action records;
- management review outputs;
- version history.

## Evidence integrity

Evidence should be attributable, dated, reproducible and protected from casual editing. Screenshots alone are weak evidence when configuration exports, system logs, CI results or signed records are available.
