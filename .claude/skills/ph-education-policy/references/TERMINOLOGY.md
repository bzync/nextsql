# Philippine Education Policy Terminology

- **DO**: DepEd Order.
- **DM**: DepEd Memorandum.
- **CMO**: CHED Memorandum Order.
- **PSG**: Policies, Standards and Guidelines, often program-specific in higher education.
- **SY**: School Year, commonly basic education.
- **AY**: Academic Year, commonly higher education.
- **MATATAG / Revised K–10**: DepEd revised K–10 curriculum rollout beginning SY 2024–2025, subject to later amendments and implementation guidance.
- **SSHS**: Strengthened Senior High School curriculum/program.
- **MELCs**: Most Essential Learning Competencies, used as a primary reference in pandemic-era learning delivery and related contexts.
- **ALS**: Alternative Learning System.
- **CG**: Curriculum Guide.

Terms can change meaning by issuance. Prefer the definition section of the controlling policy when available.
