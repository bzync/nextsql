# Bzync Product Competency Model

## Customer/problem
Evidence: interview/research synthesis, support/customer patterns, problem framing, segmentation, validated needs rather than feature requests copied literally.

## Strategy
Evidence: explicit choices and non-goals, competitive/market understanding, sequencing, coherent product thesis, clear tradeoffs.

## Discovery
Evidence: riskiest assumptions identified and tested cheaply; prototypes/spikes/experiments reduce uncertainty before full build.

## UX/product quality
Evidence: usable complete flows including empty/loading/error/permission/billing states, accessible/responsive behavior and consistent product language.

## Data
Evidence: trustworthy event definitions, data ownership, meaningful success/guardrail metrics, careful distinction between correlation and causation.

## Commercial
Evidence: pricing/packaging logic, customer value, cost-to-serve, margins, acquisition/retention implications and contract promises understood.

## Technical literacy
Evidence: can reason about APIs, data models, migration, latency, reliability, infrastructure and dependencies enough to make realistic product tradeoffs.

## Risk
Evidence: privacy/security/legal/operational consequences surfaced during discovery—not after launch.
