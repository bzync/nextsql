# Product Competency Maturity Rubric

## Level 1 — Aware
Understands vocabulary and can contribute with close guidance.

## Level 2 — Practicing
Completes bounded work using established patterns; still needs help with ambiguity/tradeoffs.

## Level 3 — Independent
Owns normal discovery-to-release work, uses evidence, anticipates downstream states and closes loops after launch.

## Level 4 — Leading
Handles complex/ambiguous product areas, improves team practices, mentors, coordinates multiple functions and recognizes second-order effects.

## Level 5 — Strategic
Creates reusable product systems/capabilities, influences company strategy and portfolio decisions, balances customer/business/technology/risk across horizons.

## Assessment rules

- score the demonstrated behavior, not job title;
- use multiple recent evidence samples;
- allow domain-specific levels rather than one averaged vanity score;
- define what evidence would justify the next level;
- reassess after meaningful work, not weekly fluctuations.
