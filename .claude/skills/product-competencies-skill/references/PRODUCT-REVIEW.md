# Product Review Checklist

## Problem
- Who experiences it?
- What evidence says it matters?
- Existing workaround/alternative?
- Desired outcome?

## Experience
- primary flow
- onboarding/discovery
- empty/loading/error/success
- permissions/roles
- mobile/responsive/accessibility
- destructive/reversible actions

## Business
- pricing/packaging impact
- direct variable costs
- support/operational load
- contractual promise
- retention/growth impact

## Technical/risk
- data/API/migration
- infrastructure/reliability
- privacy/security
- abuse/fraud
- observability/support

## Measurement
- success metric
- guardrails
- event/data quality
- rollout cohort
- decision/revisit trigger
